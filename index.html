🗑️ Reset App

Permanently deletes every photo, note, punch item, change order, RFI, submittal, safety log, clock/daily log entry, trade contact, wage rate, material expense, job budget, uploaded drawing, LeaseFlow property/unit/tenant/lease/payment record, and your AI Worker setup from this phone. This can't be undone.

🔑 LeaseFlow Storage Note

Units, tenants, leases, and payments are stored the same way as everything else in SiteWalk — localStorage, on this one device. That's fine for a single-user prototype, but a rent roll or payment ledger handed to a bookkeeper who isn't operating this browser needs storage that survives independent of one device/session. Known limitation of this pass, not solved yet.
